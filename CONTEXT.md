# Draft Picker Glossary

- **Draft Assistant** — A personal adviser that observes one fantasy football draft and gives a Recommendation to the Participant when asked. It does not submit picks.
- **Participant** — The person who owns the roster that the Draft Assistant supports.
- **Draft State** — The current picks, available Players, Participant roster, and active turn.
- **Recommendation** — One Recommended Pick followed by ordered Backup Picks.
- **Draft Score** — A relative measure used to compare available Players. It is not a prediction of win probability.
- **Calculated Pick** — The leading Player by Draft Score before Model Judgment is applied.
- **Final Recommendation** — The Recommendation after Model Judgment is applied.
- **Recommended Pick** — The available Player that is expected to give the Participant the best chance to win under the League Rules and current Draft State.
- **Backup Pick** — An available Player that the Participant can select if the Recommended Pick becomes unavailable.
- **Model Judgment** — A supplemental assessment from the conversational model. It can change a Recommendation only when the calculated evidence for the leading Players is similar.
- **Trade Offer** — A proposed exchange of Players or draft picks between the Participant and another team.
- **Confirmed Trade Offer** — A Trade Offer whose assets and giving sides the Participant has verified.
- **Trade Evaluation** — Advice about a Confirmed Trade Offer that the Draft Assistant gives only after the Participant asks for it.
- **League Rules** — The scoring rules, roster positions, draft type, keeper rules, and other settings that control the draft and season.
